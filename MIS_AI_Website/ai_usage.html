Correct AI Usage

The correct use of AI in college depends on the time and place. Every class/professor has their own conditions, written out in the syllabus, about how to use AI in the class. If used correctly it can be very helpful in studying as well as clarifying details that were taught in class. You need to be very careful in how you use AI because it is still not 100% fault free. Make sure to double check sources and what AI gives you, to ensure that it will not hurt you later.

Incorrect AI Usage

Copying/Pasting Answers
Students use AI to do their entire homework, which leads to them not learning anything.

Creating Images without citations
Some students will submit work that is purely made by AI, but eill not cite it which would be considered plagirism.

Giving it Private Information
AI has become very common now that people give it information that it should not have. This risks this info to get leaked, leading to even bigger consequences.

Theincorrect use of AI in college includes asking it to do your entire assignment or copying and pasting answers from it in your tests/ homework. When you are not actually learning anything, that is when AI use becomes an issue. It is not benefiting anyone, therefore it is wrong. The consequences for using AI incorrectly include failing the class or the assignment but also falling behind because you do not actually know the material. Overall, it could stop you from getting your dream career as it looks like you are someone that just wants to get things done, rather than using a process that can benefit you and everyone else.
